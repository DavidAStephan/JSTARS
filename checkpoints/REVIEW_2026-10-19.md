## CRITICAL — fix before anything else

### C1. The stringency series in `data.csv` is not an observed index

| quarter | stringency | Δ log GDP | note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2020Q2 | 7.316 | −6.23 | national lockdown; hours collapse |
| 2020Q3 | 63.675 | +3.34 | rebound quarter |
| 2020Q4 | 31.8375 | +2.44 | = 63.675 / 2 exactly |
| 2021Q1 | 15.91875 | +1.57 | / 2 exactly |
| 2021Q2 | 7.959375 | +1.14 | / 2 exactly |
| 2021Q3 | 3.979688 | −1.58 | Delta lockdowns (NSW/VIC) |
| 2021Q4 | 59.13211 | +3.13 | rebound quarter |
| 2022Q1 | 6.5985 | +0.98 | ≈ / 9, then / 4 exactly each quarter to 2023Q1 |

A quarterly-average Bishop–Boulter index cannot decay by exactly ½ (or
¼) to four decimal places; this is a constructed decay dummy, and it is
dated one quarter *after* each lockdown. Consequences already visible
in production:

- φ_y, φ_u are forced tiny (−0.023, −0.007) and κ_y balloons to 8.5
  because the level shifter is pushed against the data — the "d/κ
  competition" flagged in CHECKPOINT_07 and CHECKPOINT_15 was a symptom
  of this, not an identification subtlety.
- Because φ_y < 0, `y − φ_y·d` adds ≈ +1.3 pp to COVID-adjusted GDP in
  2021Q4; the smoothed gap jumps 1.20 → 1.98 that quarter. With
  φsum = 0.965 the gap is near unit root, so the artefact is carried
  into 2022–25.

**Action:** confirm the source and construction of the column with
whoever built `data.csv`; replace with the true quarterly average of
the index (brief: "Bishop–Boulter (2021) stringency index, quarterly
average, 2020Q1–2023Q4, else 0"); rerun production and every COVID A/B.
Nothing downstream of this is quotable until it is done.

### C2. r* is determined by the prior, not the data

- ν posterior −0.016, sd 0.024, 90 % band [−0.059, +0.018] — straddles
  zero against a prior N(−0.10, 0.15). ξ is therefore nearly flat in
  the likelihood.
- The r* 90 % band is ≈ ±3.4 pp at every date; P1(ξ,ξ) = 4 alone gives
  ±3.3 pp. The r* median ≈ 6.2·g^z with ξ at its prior mean. The
  reported 0.68 is "4/(1−α) × trend MFP growth", not something the
  interest-rate data inform.
- What the data do pin down is the long-run IS multiplier
  ν/(1−φsum) ≈ −0.46 — a ν–φsum ridge.

**Options (owner decision):** (a) an HLW-style truncation
ν ≤ −0.0025 (or −0.005) as a truncated normal, labelled as a choice;
(b) a pre-1993 real-rate history (RBA interbank/overnight or 90-day
bill from 1976 — the 1980s disinflation is the informative episode);
(c) report P1(ξ) sensitivity in any case.

## MAJOR

### Specification

- **M1. Eq. 17 (capital-stock drift) may be mis-transcribed.** Code
  (`ModelSpec.m:188`) matches the transcription exactly:
  g^k drift = 0.025·(1/(1−α))·(ḡ^z + ḡ^wapop). Balanced growth for the
  Cobb–Douglas trend (eq. 9) implies ḡ^z/(1−α) + ḡ^wapop. The
  transcribed form hard-wires ≈ 0.19 pp/q of extra capital deepening
  and likely drags ḡ^z down by ≈ α·ḡ^w ≈ 0.13 pp/q, which feeds r* at
  6.2× (≈ 0.5–0.8 pp). Posterior ḡ^z = 0.14 is below the ≈ 0.24 the
  2000–25 GDP identity implies. The transcription was made from photos:
  **verify against the original document**, then ≥3-seed A/B if changed.
- **M2. U-shaped Beta(0.889, 0.889) priors on ρ_U, ρ_pr, ρ_hpp, ρ_k,
  ρ_w** (chosen to reproduce Rees's mean 0.5 / sd 0.3) have infinite
  density at 0 and 1. ρ_k = 0.98 and ρ_w = 0.96 pile at the boundary,
  so rows 3 and 7 become difference equations and the levels of k*, w*
  are anchored only by P1 — harmless for τ* (z* absorbs it) but it
  makes the supply decomposition meaningless. Also eq. 34 (wapop AR(1))
  is applied to the whole sample where the spec has eq. 3 pre-COVID.
  Suggest Beta(2,2)-type priors for ρ_k, ρ_w; sensitivity on φsum prior
  (mean 0.85, sd 0.05) — a near-I(1) gap competes with the random-walk
  z* and makes COVID-era shocks persist.
- **M3. NAIRU 5.25 and the Phillips slope are shaped by `pi_e`.**
  `pi_e` runs 1.6 → 2.7 over 2020Q3–2022Q4 with measurement sd fixed
  at 0.30, so π^e tracks it and cyclical inflation is absorbed as
  "expectations"; U* is then pinned by the Okun row and its smoothness.
  γ2 = −0.218 (sd 0.056) sits on its prior mean (−0.20) with half the
  prior sd — partly prior-driven. Sensitivities: no `pi_e` row; π^e
  anchored at 2.5 post-1993. Confirm `pi_e`'s horizon — one series
  currently serves as both long-run anchor and rate deflator.

### Analysis / reproducibility

- **M4. The shipped production table has no provenance.** All three
  `per_seed` entries in `results/production/provenance.json` are
  `"missing"` (seeds predate the feature); the data-vintage and config
  checks therefore never ran on the quoted numbers; `git_commit` is
  HEAD at *pooling* time; no dirty-tree flag or diff hash is recorded.
  Regenerate once from seeds that carry provenance; add `git_dirty`.
- **M5. Quoted convergence numbers mix two configurations.**
  CLAUDE.md / README / METHODOLOGY "rank-folded R̂ ≈ 1.22, max(bulk,
  folded) ≈ 1.24 (CP14)" splices the *pre-WasteFree* trio kernel's
  folded-only figure (1.22; its proper max(bulk, folded) is 1.55) with
  the WasteFree run's 1.24 (whose folded is 1.04). No G=20 run exists
  for the exact production config (WasteFree + PoolAcuteOnly +
  ESSTargetFrac 0.7). The "~10-point LML noise floor" is stale
  (CP12/13 show 20–60). State config and G beside every R̂; run
  `benchmarks/runNeffG20.m` on the final config.
- **M6. Adoption evidence was thin for three of five sampler changes.**
  MutationTransform + StructuredBlocks (5.82 → 2.84) and MStepsLadder
  (2.84 → 2.20) were each adopted on a single 3-seed triple; CP14 later
  showed 3-seed maxima are mostly noise (`production.m:37-40` still
  credits the trio with "5.82 → 2.20"). More important: φ2, φsum, ρ_U
  shift the same direction with *every* sampler lever (CP16 finding 4)
  — a common-mode bias R̂ cannot detect. CP14's use of agreement with
  the Table-3 baseline as evidence of better mixing is circular. Report
  the lever-sensitivity of φ2/φsum/ρ_U as part of the uncertainty.
- **M7. Pooled smoothed-state bands are averages of per-seed quantiles**
  (`production.m:256-279`, Vincentisation) — *narrower* than the mixture
  band where seeds disagree, the opposite of an envelope — and nothing
  in `smoothed_states.csv` or `provenance.json` says so. Persist per-seed
  draw subsamples (or a mixture-quantile grid) in `estimate`, or at
  least stamp `states_pool_method`.

### Engineering

- **M8. Resumability can produce un-poolable "done" seeds.**
  `production` skips a seed iff `posterior_summary.csv` exists, but
  `estimate.m:338` writes that *before* `smoothed_states.csv` (341) and
  `provenance.json` (343); a kill in that window leaves a seed that
  looks finished and breaks pooling. The `particles_stage_*.mat`
  snapshots pooling needs are gitignored, so a fresh clone skips and
  then fails at `assert(~isempty(snaps))`. Treat a seed as done only if
  all outputs plus the φ=1 snapshot exist.
- **M9. Pooling logic exists in three copies** (`production.m`,
  `benchmarks/runCovidTreatment.m`, `benchmarks/poolRuns.m`) and the two
  legacy copies plus `runConvergenceCheck.m` still pick the final
  snapshot by `datenum` (`poolRuns.m:22`, `runConvergenceCheck.m:44`),
  which `production.m` itself documents as wrong after a `cp`. Extract
  one `jointstar.poolSeeds` and test it once.
- **M10. `production.m` (403 lines) has zero tests** — pooling math,
  R̂, `dataVintage`/`configMismatch` errors, skip logic all untested.

## MINOR

- −Inf from Cholesky failures (`computeLogLik.m:133,241,282`) silently
  zero-weights a particle / rejects a proposal; the failure rate has
  never been measured (`testJointstarSystem.m:63` tolerates 4/30
  non-finite prior draws). Add per-stage counters by cause; retry with
  jitter/`ldl` if H̃ fails.
- No Kalman-filter oracle covers the *full* system: A2, time-varying
  Q/R/A1, d_t/c_t for t ≥ 2 and multivariate `Zlag` are only
  "runs-and-is-finite" tested. METHODOLOGY_NOTE §3 overclaims
  (`testZlagVsCompanion` has empty A2). One companion-form test at ~5
  prior draws closes it.
- `out.lml`: the estimator is a valid normalised-prior estimate (the
  METHODOLOGY_NOTE §4.5 claim that it omits normaliser constants is
  wrong — particles start from the normalised prior and `lp` enters only
  MH ratios), but Jensen bias with a 46–59-point seed spread makes it
  unusable for comparison. Never quote it; compare by log-mean-exp over
  ≥5 seeds or not at all. No test checks the logZ *value* (the
  conjugate toys in `testWasteFree` make an analytic check ~10 lines).
- Logit Jacobian underflows at |η| ≳ 37 (`paramTransform.m:189`,
  `log1p(-s)` → −Inf) — matters for alphapi/phisum near bounds. Use
  −softplus(η) − softplus(−η) + log(hi−lo).
- `colSlice` (`computeLogLik.m:864`) and `cvec` (173/176) decide by
  `isvector`, wrong for a 1×T slice when p = 1 or m = 1 — latent,
  JointSTAR unaffected. Decide by `size(C,2) == T`.
- `priorSample.m:34` replaces κ still < 1 after 200 rejections with
  1 + 0.1·rand (not the prior). Error or count instead.
- `Zlag` silently ignored at t = 1 (`computeLogLik.m:215`); correct only
  because `loadData` masks those rows. Add an assertion.
- Cache regime grouping is derived from probe-θ values
  (`buildEvalCache.m:164-181`); a future probe collision would merge
  regimes silently. Derive from `dat` masks or verify with a second
  probe. The cache harness does not cover PoolAcuteOnly or commonKappa3.
- Prior-dominated parameters: α (posterior ≡ prior — calibrate to the
  non-farm capital share), σ_ξ (ratio 0.77), σ_gz (0.5), γ2 (0.5),
  kapHyp_* (prior-only, already noted).
- `wapop` = lf/(pr/100) is an identity in lf and pr (15+ population,
  not 15–64); its error is mechanically correlated with pr's, which a
  diagonal R ignores. Use the ABS civilian-population series.
- commonKappa3 (uncommitted): the third κ (1.85 over 2022Q1–2023Q3)
  also inflates the *inflation-row* noise during the surge that
  identifies γ2 and the NAIRU — consider exempting row 2 or using the
  `_22` window. Do not adopt until C1 is fixed.
- `postCovidPredictiveScore.m`: the joint score is a valid joint
  predictive density (the Phillips row is specified conditional on U_t,
  and U_t is itself scored); the *per-row* inflation score is
  conditional on realised U_t and should be labelled. Arms with
  different NaN masks score different conditioning sets; show paired
  differences with Monte-Carlo error, not `total`.
- `estimate.m:442` provenance `flags` omits GTrendRotation, RateGapAR,
  FixSingletonKappa, CovidDecay, DropPhiY.
- `computeLogLik.m` (874 lines) carries the dead Rfull/horseshoe path in
  both the cached and uncached bodies; six dormant flags with ~1,400
  lines of tests (GTrendRotation, RateGapAR refuted). Delete or archive.
- No validators on `estimate`'s 22 `addParameter`s; most errors are bare
  `assert`s without `jointstar:` IDs.
- `results/experiments/` (138 MB) is untracked but not ignored.
- Docs stale: CLAUDE.md "111 tests" (119); `production.m` cites a
  CLAUDE.md section "Final/production spec call" that does not exist and
  refers to `results/production_rawkernel`/`_triokernel` that are gone;
  horseshoe references in README:15,36 and the ModelSpec/buildEvalCache/
  computeLogLik headers; README "all eight checkpoints complete";
  RUNNING_ON_A_NEW_MACHINE line refs (~337/341 → 358–370) and "leave
  MSteps at default" (default 4, production 2); `computeNeff.m` header
  says production picks snapshots by datenum.
